# OSM junction indication (2026-11-14)

OSM is a topology prior, never a lane-position measurement. Connected same-level graph nodes on the matched way (degree >= 3, at least two ways, within 85 m and local forward range -20..75 m) are marked with diamonds on the GPS schematic. Nearby unrelated and grade-separated road junctions do not qualify. Three-way nodes may be a junction or merge; graph degree alone cannot distinguish them.

A main-scene label distinguishes OSM-only candidates from camera-supported passage. A sustained nearby node (same node on >=3 samples within 45 m) can replace traffic-signal support for an existing large-turn detector. The detector still requires >=35 degree host turn, missing camera longitudinal markings, continuous observations and >=4 m motion. Junction surface extent comes from host odometry and camera/vehicle road width; OSM does not set lane count, host lateral position, or a projected intersection boundary. Straight-through intersections still require the existing camera crossing-road evidence; map-only nodes do not create arbitrary road surfaces. Full branch reconstruction is not implemented.
